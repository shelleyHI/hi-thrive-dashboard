Privacy & how we use your information

Privacy & How We Use Your Information

What we collect
When you use HI Thrive, we may collect: your name, email, and phone number; menopause stage and health notes; symptom logs you enter; lab results and files you or your practitioner upload; messages you send in Thrive Chat; and notes from your consultations with your practitioner.

How it's used
This information helps your practitioner understand your health and personalise your care and coaching. Symptom trends, lab history, and chat conversations may all be reviewed by your practitioner to inform your treatment.

Who can see it
Only you and Healthy Innovations practitioners/staff involved in your care can access your information. Other clients can never see your data. We do not sell your information or share it with any third party for marketing.

How this is enforced
Your data isn't just hidden by the app's screens — it's locked at the database level itself. Even if someone had your login, they could only ever see data belonging to you. Staff access is separately verified the same way. This is enforced by the database on every request, not just by what buttons you can see.

AI assistant (Thrive Chat)
Thrive Chat drafts a response to help you — it doesn't take any action on your behalf, change your health plan, or contact your practitioner. Anything it suggests is just conversation; only you and Shelley can actually update your records. When you message Thrive, your message is sent to Anthropic (the company behind the Claude AI model) to generate that response. Anthropic processes this data to provide the response but does not use it to train their models under our current arrangement, and does not sell it.

Where it's stored
Your information is stored securely on servers located in Australia (via Supabase), with access restricted to logged-in, authorised users only.

How long we keep it
We retain your information for as long as you remain a client, and as required by Australian health record-keeping obligations after that. You can ask us to delete your account information at any time, subject to those legal record-keeping requirements.

Your rights
Under Australian privacy law, you can ask to see what information we hold about you, request corrections, or ask questions about how it's used. Contact Shelley for any of this.
